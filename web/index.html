Start reads your level and EXP, then takes your inventory: open it (I) and click through Equip, Use, Set-up and Etc, scrolling each to the bottom. A page is captured each time the view holds still; keep the mouse off the items (tooltips block the read). It finishes by itself 4 s after all four tabs have a page. While the session runs, EXP is read every few seconds. End does the same inventory pass and shows the results. Paused time doesn't count towards the per-hour rates.
